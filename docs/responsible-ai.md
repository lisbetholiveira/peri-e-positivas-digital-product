# Responsible AI Principles

Peri&Positivas explores AI-enabled research, content and product workflows in a domain that may include health-adjacent questions. That requires explicit boundaries.

## Principles

### 1. No automatic diagnosis
AI-supported content should not treat symptoms or personal experiences as proof of a medical condition.

### 2. Evidence and inference stay separate
Sourced facts, qualitative observations, strategic interpretations and hypotheses should be clearly distinguished.

### 3. Source quality matters
Health-related factual claims should rely on credible and current sources appropriate to the claim being made.

### 4. Uncertainty should remain visible
When evidence is incomplete or conflicting, the output should not manufacture certainty.

### 5. Human review for higher-stakes content
AI-generated health or other higher-stakes material requires editorial judgment before publication or use.

### 6. Privacy by design
Sensitive personal information should not be exposed in public portfolio artefacts or unnecessary workflow outputs.

## Role of AI

AI is treated as an enabling layer for research structuring, ideation, workflow acceleration and prototyping — not as a substitute for professional judgment, evidence or appropriate expert care.
