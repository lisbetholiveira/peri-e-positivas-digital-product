# Product Strategy

## Product challenge

Peri&Positivas is designed around a broad midlife user journey in which health, identity, work, confidence and life direction may overlap. The product problem is not simply lack of information; it is the difficulty of turning fragmented information into a realistic next step.

The strategic objective is to reduce uncertainty and help users progress from understanding a problem to choosing an appropriate action.

## Ecosystem model

The product ecosystem connects four layers:

1. **Audience research** — understand language, pain points, questions and barriers.
2. **Content & discovery** — help users find relevant, trustworthy information.
3. **Tools & digital products** — turn insight into practical decision support.
4. **Guided support & community** — create structured pathways beyond self-serve content.

## Product principles

- Start from a real user question rather than a format.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Design for decision support, not information volume.
- Avoid universal promises for heterogeneous midlife experiences.
- Treat trust, clarity and usability as product requirements.
- Use AI to accelerate research and workflow execution without removing human judgment.

## Discovery loop

**Listen → structure evidence → identify unmet need → form hypothesis → prototype → observe response → iterate**

This keeps product development anchored in user evidence rather than assumptions.
