# Audience Research Framework

This document explains the qualitative research method used in the project.

## Four-level analysis

1. **Observed evidence** — what was explicitly said or described?
2. **Pattern** — what repeats within the available sample?
3. **Strategic inference** — what could this imply for content, product or journey design?
4. **Hypothesis** — what should be validated before being treated as a broader truth?

## Typical research outputs

- functional and emotional pain points
- explicit and implicit questions
- barriers to action
- likely search queries and intent
- decision stage
- content and product opportunities
- validation questions

Small qualitative samples are treated as hypothesis-generating evidence, not as representative market proof.
