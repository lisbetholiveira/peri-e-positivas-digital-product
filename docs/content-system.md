# Content System

## Content as part of the product

In this project, content is not treated only as promotion. It is part of the user journey and should help a person understand a problem, reduce uncertainty and choose a useful next step.

## Content architecture

The system can include:

- search-led educational articles
- decision-support content
- newsletters and recurring editorial formats
- structured guides and workbooks
- FAQs and answer-engine-friendly formats
- internal pathways between related needs and journey stages

## Discovery principles

Content planning considers:

- user language
- search intent
- decision stage
- evidence requirements
- likely next question
- appropriate call to action

SEO, AEO and GEO are treated as discovery mechanisms, but usefulness and trust remain the primary editorial requirements.

## AI-enabled workflow

AI can support clustering, outlining, repurposing and quality-control workflows. It should not be used to invent evidence, medical claims or certainty that the source material does not support.
